The Rest Is Politics: AI Series

Will AI rewrite the global balance of power, take our jobs, or end humanity altogether - or is it just another bubble dressed in revolutionary clothes? Across nine episodes, Rory Stewart and Matt Clifford sit down with the people building, regulating, and worrying about artificial intelligence - among them Yoshua Bengio, William MacAskill, Demis Hassabis's biographer, and Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark - to ask who is really in charge of the technology reshaping our world.
